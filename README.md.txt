EduPilot AI

EduPilot AI is an AI-powered,full-stack student study-abroad copilot designed to simplify the complex journey of international education.From personalized academic roadmaps to navigating the financial hurdles of studying abroad,EduPilot AI acts as a comprehensive guide for students aiming for global universities.
A core pillar of the platform is the 'Edu Loan Hub',a dedicated module built to demystify international education financing through interactive tools,transparent cost breakdowns,and structured preparation checklists.


Key Features

Interactive EMI Calculator: Dynamic sliders allowing students to estimate monthly installments based on varying loan amounts,interest rates,and tenures.
Lender Comparison Matrix: A side-by-side view of top financial institutions,comparing interest rates,processing fees,and loan terms to maximize savings.
Financial Roadmap: A step-by-step timeline detailing transparency on total interest costs,processing overheads,and disbursement milestones.
Smart Document Checklist: A dynamic verification list categorized by applicant type(Student vs. Co-applicant/Guarantor) ensuring hassle-free loan approvals.

Other Features

AI Study-Abroad Roadmap:Personalized,step-by-step guidance for university selection,exam prep,and application timelines.


Tech Stack

Frontend: React.js,Next.js(App Router),Tailwind CSS,Lucide React(Icons)
Backend & Database: Node.js,Express.js,MongoDB (MERN Stack architecture)
Deployment: Vercel(Frontend)

Future Roadmap

AI-Driven Loan Eligibility Predictor: Integrating machine learning models to assess a student's profile(IELTS scores,academic history,co-signer income)and predict loan approval probabilities.
